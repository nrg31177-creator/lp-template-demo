Our Philosophy

Hospitality shaped by silence.

For five generations, Kōyō Ryokan has welcomed travelers seeking rest beyond the reach of the everyday. Every room opens onto the mountain, every meal follows the harvest.

Our onsen draws directly from a natural spring, unchanged since the inn's founding in 1892.
